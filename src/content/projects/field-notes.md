Field Notes explores a gentle path from capture to synthesis. Notes begin as plain text, gain links over time, and can be exported without a proprietary format.

## Design constraint

The interface has to feel useful before the notebook is large. Search, dates, and lightweight grouping provide structure without demanding a taxonomy up front.
